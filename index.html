About Me

Hello! I’m Janne Marie! A Cyber Security student with a strong interest in secure systems and backend development.

I am a Bachelor’s student in Cyber Security (degree finished summer 2026) with a solid foundation in networking, system security, and programming. Previously studied backend technologies and enjoy understanding how applications, systems, and networks function and how they can be secured against modern threats.

My goal is to contribute to meaningful projects where security, structure, and reliability are essential.
